Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import inspection locations?

From the home screen, tap Choose CSV File and select a CSV with one location name per line (e.g. one room or property item per row).

Where are my photos saved?

Photos taken with the in-app camera are saved both inside the app and to a "Property Inspection" album in your device's Photos app. Photos picked from your library are saved inside the app only.

What does "Clean Up with AI" do?

It sends the text of your transcribed voice note (never the audio itself) to an AI service to produce a polished paragraph for your report. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need an account?

No. Inspector Gadget works entirely offline except for the optional AI clean-up feature — no sign-in or account is required.
