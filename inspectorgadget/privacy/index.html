Privacy Policy

Inspector Gadget  ·  Last updated July 2026

Inspector Gadget is a utility app for property/trades inspectors to capture inspection photos by location and record voice notes. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data Storage

Photos and voice recordings you capture are stored locally on your device in the app's own storage. Photos taken with the in-app camera are also saved to a "Property Inspection" album in your device's Photos app. None of this data is uploaded to any server operated by the developer.

Voice Note Transcription & AI Clean-Up

Voice recordings are transcribed to text using Apple's Speech Recognition framework. If you tap "Clean Up with AI," the transcribed text only (never the audio, and never your photos) is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce a polished paragraph suitable for a report. This is the only data this app transmits off your device, and only happens when you explicitly request it.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Account information — no sign-in is required
  • Usage analytics or event tracking
  • Crash reports or diagnostics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location/GPS data — "locations" in the app are text labels imported from a CSV file (e.g. room or property names), not geographic coordinates

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service this app communicates with is Google's Gemini API, used solely for the optional "Clean Up with AI" voice-note feature described above. No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are used.

Children's Privacy

Inspector Gadget is a business productivity tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated date at the top of this page will reflect the change. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
