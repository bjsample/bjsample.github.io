Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your data — customers, invoices, expenses, and photos — is stored in your personal iCloud account. It syncs automatically across your devices and is backed up with iCloud. We never see or store your data on our servers.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone and select Trades Invoice Pro. You can cancel at any time and will retain Pro access until the end of the current billing period.

I lost my data after reinstalling — how do I restore it?

Your data is tied to your iCloud account. After reinstalling, sign into the same iCloud account and your data will sync back automatically. Make sure iCloud is enabled in Settings.

How do I restore my Pro subscription after reinstalling?

Open the app, trigger the upgrade screen (tap + when you've reached a free tier limit), then tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription will be restored instantly.

Can I use the app on multiple devices?

Yes. Since all data is stored in iCloud, it syncs automatically across any iPhone signed into the same Apple ID.

How do I share or send an invoice?

Open any invoice and tap the Share button. You can send the PDF via email, Messages, AirDrop, or save it to Files.
